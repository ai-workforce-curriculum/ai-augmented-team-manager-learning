# Exercise 01 — Role Scope and Level Ladder Map

**Estimated effort:** 2 hours.

## Objective

Produce a one-page map that places **your own role, today**, on the AI Workforce / AI Governance / AI Engineering ladder described in Chapter 1, and names the four nearest neighbours by role, level, and hand-off relationship. The goal is not a theoretical ladder — it is the ladder you actually sit on at your employer, with real names, real teams, and real reporting lines (or their absence).

## Prerequisites

- Chapter 1 of this module.
- The [`CURRICULUM.md`](../../../CURRICULUM.md) and [`PREREQUISITES.md`](../../../PREREQUISITES.md) of this track, for the full list of sibling roles.
- Access to your organisation's org chart or role ladder (even an informal one).

## Steps

1. **Anchor yourself on the ladder.** In one sentence, state the role you are preparing for or currently hold, and why it maps to level 40 by the Chapter 1 definition (one team, 5–30 ICs, AI-augmented work, operating-system ownership). If any of the criteria do not fit — e.g., you lead 3 people, or you lead 50, or AI augmentation is not yet central to the team's workflow — write a short note on which criterion is off and what that means for how you use the track.
2. **Identify your below-you neighbours.** Name the IC roles your reports hold (or will hold) and map each to its closest sibling track (`ai-assisted-software-engineer` L25, `ai-augmented-analyst` L20, `ai-governance-analyst` L15). Note, in one line per neighbour, what you inherit from them and what you never try to out-ship.
3. **Identify your sideways neighbours.** Name the peer-level roles you currently work with cross-functionally — AI-feature PM, risk engineer, infra-team lead, eval engineer. For each, write (a) the one thing you commission from them and (b) the one thing you consume from them.
4. **Identify your above-you neighbours.** Name the roles above you on the ladder (`ai-enablement-lead` L45, `ai-engineering-manager` L50, `senior-ai-governance-architect` L50, `head-of-ai-governance` L60, `chief-ai-officer` L70). For each that exists in your organisation, write the one-sentence hand-off ("I hand them X when Y").
5. **Produce the map.** One page. Boxes for you and the four neighbour layers (below IC, below governance, sideways peers, above). Arrows with one-sentence labels. Any gaps ("we do not have an enablement lead") explicitly marked.

## Starter guidance

- Use the Chapter 1 neighbour table as scaffolding; do not copy it verbatim — the point is to replace the generic descriptions with your real counterparts.
- If your organisation has no formal role at one of the levels, write "none; this work routes to …" and route it to whichever existing role has absorbed it (your director, your platform team, legal, a shared services function). The gap itself is a finding.
- If your organisation has multiple roles at the same level (e.g., two L45 enablement leads for different divisions), pick the one closest to your team and note the others as "parallel L45."
- Keep the map legible to someone who has never seen your org — a reviewer should be able to orient in under two minutes.

## Acceptance criteria

The map is complete when:

- Your own role is stated with a one-sentence justification against the Chapter 1 L40 definition, including any criterion that does not fit.
- All four neighbour layers (below-IC, below-governance, sideways-peers, above) are named with the real people or teams that occupy them in your org.
- Every neighbour has a one-sentence hand-off direction labelled on the arrow.
- Every absence is explicitly marked rather than silently omitted.
- The whole artifact fits on one page and is legible to an outside reviewer.

## Stretch goals

- Repeat the exercise for the role you want to hold **two years from now** and diff the two maps. The diff is a first sketch of your career plan under AI augmentation; mod-107 returns to it.
- Share the map with a peer manager and swap for a cross-check. Capture one correction you did not see on your own.
- If your organisation has an AIGP-certified partner (legal, risk, governance), show them the map and ask whether the governance-side neighbours are named correctly. Note any disagreement for revisiting after `mod-110`.
