## Chapter 2 — manager-output fundamentals as assumed baseline

The four canonical texts, in the order Chapter 2 presents them:

- Andrew S. Grove, *High Output Management*. Hachette Book Group edition: <https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/andrew-s-grove/high-output-management/9780679762881/>. Load-bearing chapters: Managerial Productivity (Part I) and Meetings — The Medium of Managerial Work (Part II).
- Camille Fournier, *The Manager's Path*. O'Reilly edition: <https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/the-managers-path/9781491973882/>. Load-bearing chapters: "Managing People," "Managing a Team," "Managing Multiple Teams."
- Will Larson, *An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management*. Stripe Press: <https://press.stripe.com/an-elegant-puzzle>. Load-bearing sections: team sizing; systems view of recurring work (planning, hiring, migration).
- Julie Zhuo, *The Making of a Manager*. Author site: <https://www.juliezhuo.com/book/manager.html>. Load-bearing chapters: on trust; on useful feedback.

Secondary references used indirectly in Chapter 2:

- Google re:Work — "Identify what makes a great manager" (Project Oxygen): <https://rework.withgoogle.com/guides/managers-identify-what-makes-a-great-manager/>.
- Amy Edmondson, *The Fearless Organization* — psychological-safety baseline, surfaced here and depth in `mod-107`/`mod-109`: <https://fearlessorganization.com/the-fearless-organization-book>.
