# mod-102 — Driving Team-Level AI Adoption and the Adoption Roadmap

**Estimated effort:** 18 hours.

## What this module does

Teaches the team manager how to author the artifact that **76% of the posting sample names as this role's primary deliverable** — the **team-level AI-adoption roadmap**. Grounds the central decision (which tasks route to AI first, which stay human-led) in Dell'Acqua et al.'s *jagged technological frontier*. Sequences the middle of the roadmap (pilot vs. rollout, opt-in vs. mandate) using Kotter + Rogers change-management framing adapted for team scope. Reads the industry-survey data (Microsoft Work Trend Index, McKinsey State of AI, GitHub Octoverse, Stack Overflow Developer Survey) as **input** to the plan, not as the plan. Sets the partner-coupling against Platform / DevEx / Product / Security, and the explicit graduation hand-off to `ai-enablement-lead` (L45). Produces the four adoption artifacts — roadmap one-pager, pilot charter, rollout decision log, exec "why this and not that" pre-read — that have to survive an exec read.

## Learning objectives

- Author a team-level AI-adoption roadmap — what to automate, in what order, with whom, and how to measure success — grounded in the 76% of the posting sample that names this as the manager's primary deliverable (R1).
- Sequence pilots vs. rollouts, opt-in vs. mandate, and communicate the change-management story to the team and leadership — using Dell'Acqua's *jagged technological frontier* framing to decide which tasks route to AI first and which stay human-led.
- Partner with Platform / DevEx / Product on shared tooling; know when a team-specific pilot needs to graduate into a Platform-owned product (hand-off to `ai-enablement-lead` at level 45).
- Read the industry-survey data (Microsoft Work Trend Index, McKinsey State of AI, GitHub Octoverse, Stack Overflow Developer Survey) as inputs to the adoption plan — not as the plan itself; keep the team-scope decision anchored in team context.
- Author adoption artifacts — the roadmap one-pager, the pilot charter, the rollout decision log, the "why this and not that" note for leadership — defensible under exec review.
- Position the ownership boundary: `ai-augmented-product-manager` (peer, L30) owns product-side AI-feature adoption; `ai-enablement-lead` (L45) takes this plan org-wide.
