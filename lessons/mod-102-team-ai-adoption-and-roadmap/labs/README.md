# Labs for mod-102-team-ai-adoption-and-roadmap (Driving Team-Level AI Adoption and the Adoption Roadmap)

Guided hands-on sessions. Each lab calibrates against specific chapters using a fixed input (a curated task set, a deliberately broken pilot charter, a deliberately broken exec pre-read) so you can test your reading against a known answer before running the corresponding exercise on your own team.

- [Lab 01 — Task Sort on a Curated Sample Team](lab-01-task-sort-on-a-curated-team.md) (60–75 min) — score twelve curated task families for a fictional platform team against the Chapter 2 six-dimension rubric and reconcile against an answer key; calibration before Exercise 01.
- [Lab 02 — Pilot Charter Red-Team on a Broken Sample](lab-02-pilot-charter-red-team.md) (60 min) — grade a deliberately broken pilot charter against Chapter 3's seven required fields and rewrite the kill-criteria and adoption-mode sections; calibration before Exercise 02.
- [Lab 03 — Exec Pre-Read Rewrite from a Broken Draft](lab-03-exec-pre-read-rewrite.md) (60–75 min) — diagnose the writing failure modes in a broken exec pre-read, rewrite the industry-context paragraph (Chapter 4 discipline) and the "why this and not that" section (Chapter 6 differentiator), and pressure-test against three exec questions; calibration before Exercise 06.
