# mod-104-productivity-measurement-and-ai-roi: Measuring Team Productivity, Cost, and AI ROI

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 16 hours

## Learning objectives

- Choose metrics that separate AI-induced gain from AI theatre — DX Core 4 as the current industry-standard framework, SPACE as the research anchor, DORA under AI-augmented conditions, flow metrics from Kersten 'Project to Product' — and know what each measures and misses (R4, 0.40 of sample, 4 in-window postings)
- Read the empirical literature as evidence for metric choice — Peng et al. (arXiv:2302.06590), Ziegler et al. (Commun. ACM) on Copilot productivity; Noy & Zhang (Science 2023) on knowledge-work generative-AI productivity; Brynjolfsson, Li, Raymond (NBER) on gain distribution across tenure; Dell'Acqua et al. (BCG+HBS) on the jagged frontier
- Track per-engineer token cost, tool-budget, adoption-vs-activation, and defend the AI tooling spend to leadership — including when to roll back
- Author the team-productivity narrative — a one-pager a VP can sign, a dashboard for ongoing review, and a 'what we got wrong last quarter' follow-up ritual
- Instrument qualitative signal alongside quantitative — retro prompts, 1:1 questions, pulse surveys — so the metric story doesn't drift into activity theatre
- Position the ownership boundary: dashboard construction mechanics live at `ai-augmented-analyst` (level 20); product-ROI framing for AI features lives at `ai-augmented-product-manager` (level 30) — this role owns the team-productivity narrative, not the plumbing
