# Labs for mod-104-productivity-measurement-and-ai-roi (Measuring Team Productivity, Cost, and AI ROI)

Guided, step-by-step hands-on sessions against curated fictional samples with answer keys. Each lab complements — does not duplicate — one or more exercises in [`exercises/`](../exercises/); the lab is a diagnostic drill on a fixed input, the exercise is an authoring task on your real team.

## Available

- [Lab 01 — Theatre Audit on a Curated Dashboard](lab-01-theatre-audit-on-a-curated-dashboard.md) — 60–75 min. Chapters 1, 2, and 6. Diagnose every Chapter-1 theatre shape and Chapter-2 first-assembly mistake on a curated eight-tile "AI Productivity" dashboard, then rewrite to the Chapter-2 five-tile shape with a known-limits sidebar. Calibrates for Exercise 01.
- [Lab 02 — DORA Composed Reading and Rollback-Trigger Drill](lab-02-dora-composed-reading-and-rollback-drill.md) — 60–75 min. Chapters 3, 6, 7, and 8. Read four quarterly snapshots of a fictional team's DORA + cost + qualitative signal; pin each to a Chapter-3 composition pattern, check Chapter-6 rollback triggers, assign the Chapter-7 decision, and rewrite one quarter's Section 4. Calibrates for Exercises 04 and 06.
- [Lab 03 — Literature Mis-citation Audit on an Exec Slide](lab-03-literature-miscitation-audit.md) — 45–60 min. Chapter 5, plus side-checks against Chapters 1, 7, and 9. Diagnose every citation error on a peer manager's cross-team slide against the five anchor studies (Peng, Ziegler, Noy & Zhang, Brynjolfsson, Dell'Acqua), then rewrite the three load-bearing citations in Chapter-5 responsible shape and compose Section 3 in Chapter-7 shape. Calibrates for Exercises 02 and 04.
