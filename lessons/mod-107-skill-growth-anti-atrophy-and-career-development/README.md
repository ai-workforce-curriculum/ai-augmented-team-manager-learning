# mod-107-skill-growth-anti-atrophy-and-career-development: Skill Growth, Anti-Atrophy, and Career Development in AI-Augmented Teams

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours

## Learning objectives

- Read the research on AI-induced skill distribution — Brynjolfsson, Li, Raymond (NBER): AI gains concentrate at lower tenure; Noy & Zhang (Science): generative AI compresses the top-to-bottom gap; Dell'Acqua et al.: AI-augmented workers in some tasks defer to AI and deskill — and translate each finding into a team-level policy (R10, 0.08 in postings but authoritative-research-anchored)
- Design deliberate-practice carve-outs that keep ICs sharp at the tasks AI is strong at — paired-review rotations, periodic AI-off work, judgment-weighted review standards
- Adjust the team's career-development ladder to reward judgment, not just AI-assisted throughput — what a promotion case looks like when the IC's output is co-authored with an agent
- Instrument burnout and over-reliance signals — Edmondson 'The Fearless Organization' + Westrum culture typology provide the diagnostic vocabulary; instrument them in 1:1s and retros rather than survey theatre
- Position the ownership boundary: HR formal processes (PIP, termination) link out to HR partners; this module covers the manager-side framing
