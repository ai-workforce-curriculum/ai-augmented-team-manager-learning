# Exercise 05 — IC Development Plan Template Refresh

**Estimated effort:** 2 hours.

## Objective

Refresh **the Individual Development Plan (IDP) template** your team uses in quarterly or bi-annual 1:1s so that an IC's growth plan under AI augmentation names the *judgement muscle* the IC is building and the specific artifact that will evidence the growth — rather than naming only the tool-fluency or scope goals the pre-AI template assumed. The artifact is a 2-page IDP template plus a one-page facilitator's guide for the 1:1 conversation that produces it.

This is the artifact that operationalises the Chapter 6 ladder and the Chapter 5 carve-outs into each IC's growth story. The ladder is the standard; the carve-outs are the practice; the IDP is where the specific growth happens.

## Prerequisites

- [Chapter 1](../01-the-atrophy-question-and-why-this-module-exists.md) — the module's framing on why the growth-plan is the load-bearing artifact for junior ICs under AI augmentation.
- [Chapter 5](../05-deliberate-practice-carve-outs.md) — the carve-out schedule the IDP's growth milestones compose with.
- [Chapter 6](../06-career-ladder-under-ai-augmentation.md) — the three new axes the IDP's growth milestones should map against.
- [Exercise 02 — Deliberate-practice carve-out schedule](exercise-02-deliberate-practice-carve-out-schedule.md) and [Exercise 03 — Career ladder under AI augmentation](exercise-03-career-ladder-under-ai-augmentation.md) at least in draft. The IDP depends on both.
- Your team's or organisation's existing IDP template, if one exists. If no template exists, the exercise produces v0.1 as the first version.
- A functioning quarterly or bi-annual 1:1 cadence that includes a growth conversation. If growth conversations happen only at annual review time, the IDP won't be run at the cadence this module needs.

## Problem statement

The traditional IDP template was designed around a stable model of IC growth: *"what new scope, skill, or exposure does the IC need in the next 6 months to be ready for the next level?"* The template produces growth milestones like *"lead a cross-team project"* or *"author one design doc with peer review."*

Under AI augmentation, those milestones are still valid but are no longer sufficient. Brynjolfsson (Chapter 2) shows the junior-IC growth curve is now mediated by AI in a way the old milestones did not anticipate; Dell'Acqua (Chapter 4) shows the deference risk is on outside-frontier work in ways that the old milestones did not name. The refreshed IDP adds a *judgement growth* axis that (a) names the specific judgement capacity the IC is building this cycle, (b) ties it to a Chapter 5 carve-out artifact that will evidence the growth, and (c) anchors it to a specific axis on the Chapter 6 ladder.

The refresh is not a replacement for the traditional IDP — the scope, skill, and exposure milestones stay. It is additive: one new section, one change to the artifact list, one amendment to the 1:1 facilitator's guide.

## Steps

1. **Pull your team's current IDP template.** If your organisation has a company-wide template, start with that. If your team has its own, start with that. Paste the existing structure into a draft document; your work is to layer on.
2. **Add the "Judgement growth under AI augmentation" section.** The section has four fields:
   - *Which ladder axis is this cycle's growth focused on?* Pick one of the Chapter 6 axes (A, B, or C) per cycle. Picking more than one dilutes the growth plan.
   - *What is the specific judgement capacity the IC is building?* One sentence. Not *"improve judgement"*; something like *"learn to identify when the AI's architectural suggestion on migration-shaped tasks does not fit the team's constraints, and write the alternative."*
   - *Which Chapter 5 carve-out artifact evidences the growth?* Named artifact: an AI-off artifact, a specific paired-review slot, a specific judgement-weighted review pass. The artifact is on the Exercise 02 schedule; the IDP points at it.
   - *How will we know the growth has happened?* One or two observable signs. *"IC can describe one case in a 1:1 where they would have taken a different approach than the AI; the paired-review conversation on migration-shaped tasks shows the IC leading, not following."*
3. **Modify the existing "representative artifact" section.** Add an annotation field: *"what role did AI play in producing this artifact, and what was the IC's judgement move on it?"* The field aligns the IDP artifact-section with the Chapter 6 promotion-packet template.
4. **Revise the 1:1 facilitator's guide.** One page. Three sections:
   - *Pre-meeting prep* (the manager reviews the prior cycle's IDP, the Chapter 5 carve-out outcomes for this IC, and the ladder axis target).
   - *During the conversation* (the manager asks the IC to self-assess on their judgement growth first, before the manager shares their assessment). The order matters: IC-first builds psychological safety (Chapter 7); manager-first anchors the conversation on the manager's view.
   - *Closing the meeting* (the next cycle's judgement-growth focus, the paired carve-out artifact, the check-in cadence between now and the next IDP review).
5. **Walk one IC's current growth plan through the refreshed template.** Pick one IC (one you work with regularly and whose growth arc you know). Translate their current growth plan through the refreshed template; identify where the new section adds signal and where it feels redundant with the existing growth milestones. Iterate on the template based on the walk-through.
6. **Co-review with one senior IC and one junior IC.** Senior IC: *"does this feel like growth, or does it feel like performance evaluation masquerading as growth?"* Junior IC: *"if your manager used this template, would you feel helped or audited?"* The co-review is where the psychological-safety signal lives or does not live. Record and iterate.
7. **Add the fallback: what to do when the IC's judgement-growth focus is unclear.** A short paragraph (~1/3 of a page) naming the manager's response when the IC cannot articulate a judgement-growth focus — *"let's name the task-type where you felt most dependent on the AI this cycle, and build the next cycle's focus around that."* This defends against the template producing empty judgement-growth sections when the signal has not formed yet.
8. **Soft-launch for one IC cycle.** Use the refreshed template in your next IDP cycle with one or two ICs (not the whole team). Note which fields were easy to fill, which were awkward, which produced genuine growth signal. Revise; publish to the team for the following cycle.

## Starter guidance

- **The judgement-growth section is a *section*, not a replacement.** The scope, skill, and exposure growth milestones from the pre-AI template remain. The refresh adds signal; it does not remove signal.
- **One judgement capacity per cycle.** Trying to grow judgement on three different task-shapes in one quarter is not realistic. Pick one; grow it; next cycle picks the next.
- **Tie to a specific carve-out artifact — not a vague artifact.** The IDP's judgement-growth plan points at a named artifact on the Exercise 02 schedule. If the IDP cannot point at the specific artifact, the growth plan is not operationalisable; revise the artifact to match.
- **IC-first self-assessment.** The order in the 1:1 (IC self-assesses first, manager responds) is load-bearing for psychological safety. If the manager pre-anchors with their view, the IC performs; if the IC leads, the IC's view is the baseline the manager works from.
- **The template is a tool, not a contract.** An IDP is a living plan; mid-cycle course-correction is normal. The template should accommodate updates, not freeze the cycle's plan in stone.
- **Avoid "AI collaboration skills" as a growth milestone.** It is uncitable, unobservable, and unactionable. Pick a specific judgement capacity tied to a specific task-type the team routes outside-frontier.
- **The facilitator's guide is for *you*, not the IC.** The IC sees the template; the facilitator's guide helps you run the conversation well. Do not force the IC to read the facilitator's guide.
- **If an IC pushes back on the judgement-growth section, listen.** The pushback usually reveals that the template's language is not landing well, or that the IC does not yet have a mental model for *what judgement under AI augmentation means for them*. Both are solvable; use the pushback as signal for revision.

## Acceptance criteria

The refreshed IDP template is complete when:

- The existing scope/skill/exposure sections of the IDP are preserved intact.
- A new "Judgement growth under AI augmentation" section is present with four fields (ladder axis, specific capacity, carve-out artifact, observable signs).
- The "representative artifact" section has the judgement-move annotation field.
- The 1:1 facilitator's guide is present (one page; three sections: pre-meeting prep, during the conversation, closing the meeting).
- A fallback paragraph describes the manager's response when the IC's judgement-growth focus is unclear.
- One walked-through example on an actual IC's growth plan is in the change-log.
- One cycle's soft-launch feedback is in the change-log (which fields worked, which were revised).
- A change-log entry naming the senior-IC and junior-IC reviewers and the corrections produced.
- Length: 2 pages for the template, 1 page for the facilitator's guide. Longer is over-written; the IDP needs to be fillable in a 45-minute 1:1.
- A manager running their first refreshed IDP next cycle can open the template and run the conversation without prior training.

## Stretch goals

- **A junior-tier IDP variant.** Author a junior-specific version of the template with simplified language and more scaffolding on the judgement-growth section. Junior ICs often have less mental model for *what judgement under AI augmentation looks like for me*; a scaffolded template helps.
- **A senior-tier IDP variant.** Author a senior-specific version that emphasises Axis B (mentorship of judgement) and Axis C (review craft). The variant's growth milestones often look like *"I will pair with junior IC X on migration-shaped tasks for the next cycle and visible growth in their Axis A artifact at quarter end will be evidence."*
- **Compose with the Exercise 02 carve-out schedule.** For each IC, align their IDP's judgement-growth artifact with their Chapter 5 Carve-out 2 AI-off artifact for the quarter. The two should be the same artifact; the IDP names *why*, and Exercise 02 schedules *when*.
- **Compose with the Exercise 04 prompt bank.** For each IC's judgement-growth focus, identify one Category-1 or Category-2 prompt that is especially apt. The manager's 1:1 rhythm can then route the right prompt to the right IC's growth arc.
- **A self-assessment tool.** Author a short (1-page) self-assessment the IC fills out before the IDP review — three open-text fields tied to the three ladder axes, asking the IC to narrate their own growth. The self-assessment is a psychological-safety move: the IC owns the first word.
- **Review-of-review for IDPs.** Once per year, trade IDPs with a peer manager (anonymised if your organisation prefers) and discuss where the growth plans look sharpened and where they look under-specified. The peer-read catches the tendency for the manager's own blind spots to carry into every IDP they write.
