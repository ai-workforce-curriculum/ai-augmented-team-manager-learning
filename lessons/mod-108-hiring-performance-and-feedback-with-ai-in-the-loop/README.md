# mod-108-hiring-performance-and-feedback-with-ai-in-the-loop: Hiring, Performance Management, and Feedback with AI in the Loop

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 16 hours

## Learning objectives

- Redesign the interview loop for AI-augmented candidates — when AI use is allowed, how to assess underlying capability, how to score live-coding or live-writing when the candidate has agents on hand; grounded in Google re:Work's structured-interview reference and SHRM AI-in-HR practice guidance
- Adjust levelling / promotion criteria so AI-amplified throughput doesn't crowd out judgment, system understanding, mentorship, and ownership — ties back to mod-107's career-ladder work
- Run performance management with AI in play — 1:1 cadence, feedback, goal-setting (OKRs or KPIs) that reflects AI leverage; underperformance root-cause framing (is it human, workflow, or tooling?); PIP contracts when needed, in coordination with HR partners
- Comply with AI-in-hiring legal framing — US EEOC guidance on automated employment decisions, NYC Local Law 144 bias audits, EU AI Act Article 6 + Annex III on high-risk hiring AI — enough to set team practice, not legal opinion (deferred to legal counsel)
- Author workforce-planning artifacts at team scope — headcount justification, levelling rationale, role-mix shifts — and know when to send scope upward to `ai-enablement-lead` (level 45) or `ai-engineering-manager` (level 50) (R9 covered at team-scope only)
