# Labs for mod-108-hiring-performance-and-feedback-with-ai-in-the-loop (Hiring, Performance Management, and Feedback with AI in the Loop)

The labs are guided practice on fixed, curated scenarios. They **complement** the exercises (which ask you to author the artifact for *your own* team) by letting you calibrate your reading on deliberately composed cases before the stakes become real on your team's hires, your team's ICs, and your team's vendors.

Run order is suggested; each lab stands alone.

- [Lab 01 — Rater Calibration on a Disclosure-Stage Live-Coding Transcript](lab-01-rater-calibration-on-a-disclosure-stage-live-coding-transcript.md) — 75–90 min. Score two mock candidates on the same representative problem under the Chapter 2 five-criterion rubric (problem decomposition, agent use quality, narration, debugging / recovery, communication). Catches the single most common new-rater drift Chapter 2 flags — scoring silence on the narration criterion at the middle of the scale rather than *down*. Chapters: Chapter 2 primary; side-checks Chapter 3, Chapter 6, Chapter 8. Complements [Exercise 01](../exercises/exercise-01-interview-loop-redesign-under-ai.md).
- [Lab 02 — Root-Cause Drill on Three Composite IC Cases](lab-02-root-cause-drill-on-three-composite-ic-cases.md) — 75–90 min. Run Chapter 5's six-question drill in strict order on three composite cases — a workflow + enablement + pairing composition, a not-an-underperformance-case pattern, and a human-primary Dell'Acqua deference case with small operational residuals. Calibrates the ordering (tooling → workflow → human) that Chapter 5 names as load-bearing. Chapters: Chapter 5 primary; side-checks Chapter 4, Chapter 8, and the compose points to mod-105 / mod-106 / mod-107. Complements [Exercise 04](../exercises/exercise-04-underperformance-root-cause-drill.md).
- [Lab 03 — Compliance Triage on a Vendor Intake](lab-03-compliance-triage-on-a-vendor-intake.md) — 60–75 min. Triage four hypothetical hiring AI vendor demos (interview-transcript scorer, interviewer-note summariser, video personality analysis, reference-check drafting) under Chapter 6's three load-bearing questions and classify each against US EEOC / Title VII + ADA, NYC Local Law 144, and EU AI Act Article 6 + Annex III. Catches the specific manager-side drift of approving a tool ahead of the compliance review and of under-weighting ADA exposure on video / speech / facial-analysis tools. Chapters: Chapter 6 primary; side-checks Chapter 2, Chapter 8. Complements [Exercise 05](../exercises/exercise-05-ll144-eeoc-compliance-checklist-for-your-team.md).
