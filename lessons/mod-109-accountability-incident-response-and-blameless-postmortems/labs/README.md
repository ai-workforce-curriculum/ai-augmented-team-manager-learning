# Labs for mod-109-accountability-incident-response-and-blameless-postmortems (Accountability, AI-Incident Response, and Blameless Post-Mortems)

The labs are guided practice on fixed, curated scenarios. They **complement** the exercises (which ask you to author the artifact for *your own* team) by letting you calibrate your reading on deliberately composed cases before the stakes become real on your team's next live incident.

Run order is suggested; each lab stands alone.

- [Lab 01 — Triage Drill on Four Composite AI-Incident Vignettes](lab-01-triage-drill-on-four-composite-ai-incident-vignettes.md) — 75–90 min. Triage four composite vignettes (one per Chapter 4 class, two of them composed) against the Chapter 2 severity ladder, the Chapter 4 picker, and the Chapter 5 trigger lines. Catches the two specific reading drifts the module flags — over-routing pure-quality Class 1 incidents to SecOps because the symptom sounds "customer-facing," and under-weighting active regressions caught by eval as Sev-4 near-misses when the agent is still live-serving. Chapters: Chapter 2, Chapter 4, Chapter 5 primary; side-checks Chapter 1 scope boundary. Complements [Exercise 01](../exercises/exercise-01-team-incident-runbook-authoring.md).
- [Lab 02 — Fault-Chain Walk and Blameless-Language Calibration](lab-02-fault-chain-walk-and-blameless-language-calibration.md) — 75–90 min. Rewrite six deliberately flawed sentences from a shipped post-mortem draft (first-story framing, anthropomorphising the agent, Layer-5 stop-words) into Chapter 3's outside-in systemic framing; walk the five fault-chain layers on the underlying stale-context Class 1 incident; answer the five systemic questions with specific destination artifacts. Catches the Chapter 3 contamination modes — "the model hallucinated," "the agent decided to...," "the reviewer missed it," "we need to not trust the agent" — that leak through most first-pass drafts. Chapters: Chapter 3 primary; side-checks Chapter 2 Step 4 and Chapter 6 ticket shape. Complements [Exercise 02](../exercises/exercise-02-blameless-postmortem-adapted-for-ai.md).
- [Lab 03 — Backfeed-Ticket Conversion from a Post-Mortem's Section 7](lab-03-backfeed-ticket-conversion-from-a-post-mortem.md) — 45–60 min. Convert the four free-text action items of a shipped Section 7 into Chapter 6 backfeed tickets (title, source, destination artifact as a specific file path, named human owner, verifiable close-out criterion, date); demonstrate the eval row's **red → green → gate** plan; identify the two Chapter 6 destinations the shipped write-up missed (the eval row Chapter 6's 100% incident-to-eval target depends on, and the HITL checkpoint that Section 3 Layer 3 names but Section 7 does not produce). Chapters: Chapter 6 primary; side-checks Chapter 2 Step 7, Chapter 3 Section 7, Chapter 5 handoff-vs-ticket distinction. Complements [Exercise 05](../exercises/exercise-05-regression-fixture-backfeed-ritual.md).
