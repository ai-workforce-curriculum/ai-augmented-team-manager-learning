# mod-112-cross-functional-leadership-and-ritual-calendar: Cross-Functional Leadership and the Team Ritual Calendar

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours

## Learning objectives

- Design the team ritual calendar for AI-augmented work — planning, review, retro, demo, 1:1s, office hours, community-of-practice cadence — and justify every ritual against the measurement framework from mod-104 (Lencioni 'Death by Meeting' for the trade-offs)
- Partner with Product, Platform / DevEx, Design, Data, and AI-Governance peers — know the ritual cadence and decision rights for each partnership; use Google re:Work manager-behaviour findings as the baseline
- Communicate upward to leadership — exec pre-reads, board-adjacent decks (not board-level, which lives at `head-of-ai-governance` level 60 and `chief-ai-officer` level 70), quarterly reviews; connect the AI-adoption roadmap (mod-102), productivity narrative (mod-104), governance posture (mod-110), and incident history (mod-109) into one coherent story
- Run the team's one-pager and decision-log hygiene so the team's AI-augmented operating system is legible — a new manager inheriting the team can pick up without re-discovery
- Position the IAPP AIGP certification as the shared cross-functional vocabulary; identify the other certifications / credentials (e.g. ISO/IEC 42001 lead-implementer where team evidence feeds into an AIMS program) that are load-bearing in this role's market
